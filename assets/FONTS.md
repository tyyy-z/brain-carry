# Cabinet Grotesk

Cabinet Grotesk by Indian Type Foundry, provided by Fontshare.

Files: cabinet-grotesk-400.woff2, cabinet-grotesk-500.woff2, cabinet-grotesk-800.woff2.

Source: https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/cabinet-grotesk

License information: https://www.fontshare.com/licenses

These font files are used to render the BrainCarry website and its public promotional artwork. Font credit is also displayed in the page footer.

## Noto Sans SC

Chinese glyphs use a local variable-weight Noto Sans SC subset from Google Fonts, containing the public translations' 382 non-ASCII characters. Latin characters keep Cabinet Grotesk. The font works offline and makes no runtime requests to Google.

Source: https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+SC

License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, included at fonts/OFL-NotoSansSC.txt, downloaded from https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/notosanssc/OFL.txt .

When adding new Chinese copy, regenerate the subset with appropriate font tooling or use a complete licensed CJK font. Uncovered characters fall back to the device's Chinese font.
